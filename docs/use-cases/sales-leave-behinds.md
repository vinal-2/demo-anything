# Sales and pre-sales leave-behinds

**Who it's for:** Solution engineers and account teams who want prospects to explore after a call.

## The problem

After a demo call, prospects forget where things were, and booking another call slows the deal down.

## How Demo Anything helps

- Send a link to an interactive walkthrough of exactly what you showed.
- Prospects click through on their own time, on desktop or phone, and can switch narrator voice.
- Build a version per audience, for example security buyers or admins, from the same screenshots.

## Steps

1. Capture the flow you demo most often.
2. Tailor the narration to the buyer.
3. Publish to a private or public static host.
4. Share the link in your follow-up email.

See [getting started](../getting-started.md) and [building flows with an agent](../agent-workflow.md)
for the details, and [redaction](../redaction.md) before you publish anything real.
