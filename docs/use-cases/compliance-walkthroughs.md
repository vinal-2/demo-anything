# Compliance and audit walkthroughs

**Who it's for:** Governance, risk and compliance teams preparing evidence.

## The problem

Auditors ask where a control is configured, and screenshots pasted into documents are hard to follow and easy to leak.

## How Demo Anything helps

- Show exactly where each control lives in the admin portal, step by step.
- Redaction and the sensitive-text check keep tenant details out of what you share.
- Flows are versioned in git, so you can show how a control looked at audit time.

## Steps

1. Capture the path to each control.
2. Add your organisation's names to `capture/sensitive-terms.txt`.
3. Validate and share the link or the static files.

See [getting started](../getting-started.md) and [building flows with an agent](../agent-workflow.md)
for the details, and [redaction](../redaction.md) before you publish anything real.
