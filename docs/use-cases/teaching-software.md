# Teaching software

**Who it's for:** Lecturers, bootcamps and corporate trainers.

## The problem

Students fall behind during live software demos, and recordings do not let them practise the clicks.

## How Demo Anything helps

- Give students a self-paced walkthrough that waits for them to click the right place.
- Embed it in your learning platform; it posts progress and completion events to the host page.
- Captions and keyboard support make it usable for more students.

## Steps

1. Capture the exercise in a training environment.
2. Write narration in plain language.
3. Embed with `?embed` in your course page.
4. Listen for `demo:complete` events to record completion.

See [getting started](../getting-started.md) and [building flows with an agent](../agent-workflow.md)
for the details, and [redaction](../redaction.md) before you publish anything real.
