# Bug reproduction

**Who it's for:** QA testers and engineers.

## The problem

Bug reports with "click here, then there" are ambiguous, and screen recordings are long.

## How Demo Anything helps

- Attach a short walkthrough that shows each step to reproduce, with the hotspot on the exact control.
- Engineers step through it at their own pace and see every screen.

## Steps

1. Capture the steps that trigger the bug.
2. Redact anything private.
3. Link the flow from the bug report.

See [getting started](../getting-started.md) and [building flows with an agent](../agent-workflow.md)
for the details, and [redaction](../redaction.md) before you publish anything real.
