# SaaS product tours

**Who it's for:** Founders and product marketers who want an interactive tour on their website.

## The problem

A video tour is passive and hard to update; hosted demo tools add another subscription and another place your product screenshots live.

## How Demo Anything helps

- Make a click-through product tour from screenshots of your app, with a natural voice and captions.
- Host it as static files on GitHub Pages or your own site; there is no per-viewer pricing.
- Keep the tour in git next to your docs, and update a single screen when the UI changes.
- Embed it in your landing page with an iframe.

## Steps

1. Capture the five or six screens that show your product's value.
2. Write one short narration line per step.
3. Pick a narrator in the voice sampler.
4. Publish and embed with `?embed`.

See [getting started](../getting-started.md) and [building flows with an agent](../agent-workflow.md)
for the details, and [redaction](../redaction.md) before you publish anything real.
