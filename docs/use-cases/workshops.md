# Workshops and internal talks

**Who it's for:** Anyone presenting software to a group.

## The problem

Live demos fail, and attendees cannot repeat the steps afterwards.

## How Demo Anything helps

- Present from the walkthrough instead of a live system, with no risk of a broken demo.
- Share the same link afterwards as a self-paced follow-up.
- Switch narrator voice, or turn sound off and narrate yourself.

## Steps

1. Capture the demo before the session.
2. Present it full screen.
3. Share the link with attendees.

See [getting started](../getting-started.md) and [building flows with an agent](../agent-workflow.md)
for the details, and [redaction](../redaction.md) before you publish anything real.
