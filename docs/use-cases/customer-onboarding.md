# Customer onboarding and help-centre how-tos

**Who it's for:** Support and customer-success teams answering "where is that setting?" questions.

## The problem

Written help articles with static screenshots are skimmed and misread; the same questions keep arriving.

## How Demo Anything helps

- Turn each common question into a short guided walkthrough: one click per step, the hotspot on the exact control.
- Link it from the help article or send it in a support reply.
- Viewers can use the keyboard alone or a screen reader, with captions on.

## Steps

1. List your top ten "how do I…" tickets.
2. Capture one flow for each.
3. Validate and publish.
4. Add the links to your help centre and saved replies.

See [getting started](../getting-started.md) and [building flows with an agent](../agent-workflow.md)
for the details, and [redaction](../redaction.md) before you publish anything real.
